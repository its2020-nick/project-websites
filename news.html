Term 1 2027 Begins Monday 4 January — Get Ready!

As communicated by the Ministry of Education, Term 1 of the 2027 academic year opens on Monday 4 January 2027. All learners are expected to report by 7:30 AM in full uniform. Half term runs from 19 to 28 February, and the term closes on Friday 9 April 2027. School fees must be cleared before or on the first day. Parents are welcome to accompany new learners to their classes.
