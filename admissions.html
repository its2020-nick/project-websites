Term 1 2027 Admissions — Wekan Kasarani School, Mwiki

Now enrolling PP1 to Grade 9 and Daycare for families in Mwiki, Kasarani and nearby estates. Term 1 opens Monday 4 January 2027.

Home Admissions
Admissions for Term 1 2027 are open. Learners report on Monday 4 January 2027 by 7:30 AM. Spaces are limited in each class, so we advise parents in Mwiki and Kasarani to apply early. We also accept learners during the year when space allows.

Why Mwiki & Kasarani Parents Choose Wekan

CBC from PP1 to Grade 9

Pre-Primary, Lower and Upper Primary and Junior School (Grades 7–9) in one school on Mwiki Road, following the KICD Competency Based Curriculum.

Daycare from Age 1

Full-day and half-day care, 7:00 AM – 5:00 PM, including specialised care for children with autism up to 10 years.

Hands-on Science

Practical science lessons in our school laboratory, plus a STEM club, so learners do more than read about experiments.

Safe & Supervised

CCTV-monitored classrooms and caregivers who keep parents updated on each child's progress.

Term 1 2027 Admission Checklist: Step by Step

1

Call or Visit Us

Call 0714 158 488 or 0727 156 586, or visit the office on Mwiki Road (Mon–Fri, 7:00 AM–5:00 PM) to confirm a space in your child's class.

2

Get the Application Form

Collect the form from the school secretary, or request it by phone and we will send it to you.

3

Prepare Documents

Fill in the form and gather every document in the list below, including your child's birth certificate and NEMIS UPI number.

4

Assessment / Interview

Learners joining PP1 and above sit a short assessment and interview so we place them in the right class.

5

Receive Admission Letter

Once your child is admitted, you receive the admission letter, the 2027 fee structure and joining instructions (including the uniform list).

6

Pay Fees

Pay using only the official school payment details printed on the fee structure. Keep your receipt or M-Pesa message. Fees must be cleared on or before the first day.

7

Report on 4 January 2027

Bring your child in full uniform by 7:30 AM on Monday 4 January 2027. Parents are welcome to walk new learners to class.

Documents to Bring

Birth Certificate

Copy of the learner's birth certificate, with the original for verification.

NEMIS UPI Number

The learner's Unique Personal Identifier from NEMIS if they have been registered at another school. New PP1 learners are registered by the school.

Assessment Number (Grades 3–9)

Learners transferring into Grades 3–9 must have their KNEC assessment number.

Previous School Report

Most recent report form for learners transferring into Grades 2–9.

Immunisation Card

Up-to-date immunisation / vaccination card (PP1, PP2 and Daycare).

Passport Photos

2 recent passport-size colour photos of the learner.

Parent / Guardian ID

Copy of the National ID or passport of the parent or legal guardian.

Parent FAQs: Admissions in Mwiki & Kasarani

Term 1 2027 opens on Monday 4 January 2027, and learners report by 7:30 AM. Half term runs from 19 to 28 February and the term closes on Friday 9 April 2027. We accept applications until each class is full, and also during the year when space allows, so apply as early as you can.

We admit learners from PP1 to Grade 9, plus Daycare for children aged one year and above. Children joining PP1 should be at least 4 years old by 1 January 2027, and PP2 learners at least 5 years old.

We do not currently run a school bus. The school is on Mwiki Road in Kasarani, and many families drop off their children or use the public transport that serves Mwiki Road. Our office can advise you on the best route from your estate.

Daycare children can be given nutritious snacks and meals as an optional service. For PP1 to Grade 9, the office will explain the current meal arrangements and any costs when you collect the 2027 fee structure.

All learners wear the school uniform in our colours, yellow and dark navy. The full uniform list, including where to buy it, is in the joining instructions you receive with the admission letter. Learners should report in full uniform on 4 January 2027.

Daycare is open Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 5:00 PM, with full-day and half-day options. It accepts children from one year old, including children with autism up to 10 years of age. See our Daycare page for details.

Yes. We teach the full CBC from Pre-Primary to Junior School (Grades 7–9). Junior School subjects include Integrated Science, Mathematics, Computer Science, Pre-Technical Studies, Business Studies and Creative Arts. Learners do practical work in our science laboratory and can join clubs such as STEM, debate, football and athletics. See our Curriculum page for the full subject list.

If your child is moving from another school, bring their NEMIS UPI number so we can transfer their record. Learners joining Grades 3–9 must also have their KNEC assessment number. Children starting PP1 for the first time do not need a UPI number, because the school registers them on NEMIS using their birth certificate.
